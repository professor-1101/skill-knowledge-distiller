# consolidate (R2) — adjudicate the merge candidates

Role: consolidator. Source access: **no**.

For each candidate pair: the same rule stated differently, or genuinely
distinct?

**The discriminator is the condition.** Two claims with identical advice under
different applicability conditions are two rules, and merging them destroys
exactly the knowledge worth keeping. Under genuine uncertainty prefer the
reversible move: keeping both costs one redundant rule, a bad merge silently
destroys a distinction.

When you do merge, the survivor inherits **all** the evidence. That inheritance
is where multi-source corroboration comes from, and corroboration is the
primary input to confidence.

Every claim you touch gets a disposition. `not-transferable` and `platitude`
require a non-empty reason, because those two are where a lazy pass quietly
discards the corpus.
